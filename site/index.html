      FAQ

      What is Gravit?

      An AI workbench for investment research, with an account its agents can act from.

      What is the Account?

      Your real exchange, broker, wallet and prediction-market accounts on one page. Agents trade, move money and pay inside limits you sign; you watch, steer and approve.

      Is this real money?

      When you run it, yes: real orders at the accounts you connect, each under a cap you set, with a read-only switch. The code has only ever been exercised against stand-ins.

      Where does it run?

      On your own machine, for one operator. Keys stay in your files, the owner is a key in your browser, and nothing is hosted.
